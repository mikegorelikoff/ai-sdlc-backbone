# Skills by lifecycle

Use this page when you know the delivery stage but not the exact skill. The
[searchable catalog](skills.md) remains canonical for the complete inventory
and each skill's generated reference page.

## Explore and refine

Working-backwards discovery, business analysis, goal/capability mapping,
requirements readiness, backlog review, delivery synthesis, and story
decomposition clarify what and why before implementation planning.

## Specify and plan

Architecture, specification-driven development, test-case derivation, QA
strategy, release slicing, project context, and workflow planning connect
requirements to executable tasks.

## Implement and verify

Branching, bounded implementation, validation, security testing, code review,
change impact, and commit preparation produce and assess the change.

## Govern and recover

Approvals, policy, package trust, evidence, delivery graph, runtime, doctor,
retrospective, and recovery skills protect authority and continuity.

## Handoff

Readiness and handoff reviews verify that the next person or stage receives
the required artifacts, decisions, evidence, and open risks.

For role-oriented discovery, open [skills by role](skills-by-role.md). For
exact names and contracts, search the [skill catalog](skills.md).
