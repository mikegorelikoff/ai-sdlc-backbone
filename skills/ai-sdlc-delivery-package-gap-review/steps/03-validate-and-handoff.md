# Validate and Handoff — ai-sdlc-delivery-package-gap-review: Delivery Package Gap Review

> Selector: validate, handoff, or complete

## Entry

Enter after execution has produced the expected artifact, code, plan, decision, or diagnostic evidence.

## Procedure

## Completion Criteria

- Core actors and outcomes are clear.
- Delivery blockers are identified and either clarified or explicitly left open with impact.
- The package is specific enough to support story decomposition without fiction.

## Exit

Report outcome, validation evidence, unresolved risks, and the next required or optional owner directly in the active response.
